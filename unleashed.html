The Album

WARNING: You are going to have a favorite song on this album you never expected to love.

UNLEASHED is fifteen tracks built to catch you off guard. One minute you’re dancing. The next you’re laughing. Then a line hits hard enough to shut the room up. There’s bacon, bad behavior, loyalty, aging, loss, irresistible hooks, dangerous double meanings — and a couple of tracks that definitely change after dark.

Some songs are told from a dog’s point of view. None of them are novelty songs. This is real music with teeth: funny when it wants to be, sexy when it feels like it, and devastating when you least expect it.

Press play. Pick your favorite. Then try explaining why that was the one that got you.

Fifteen songs. Zero leash. The DogMother — UNLEASHED.
