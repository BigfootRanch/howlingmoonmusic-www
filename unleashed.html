The Album

Fifteen tracks. One rule: no leash. UNLEASHED runs from whole-family doggy dance crazes to desert-disco anthems, from a rescue dog’s question at the kennel wire to the goodbye song for the ones waiting at the rainbow bridge — with a couple of after-dark tracks for when the dogs are asleep.

Every word, every hook, every bark: original. Written by Valentina Talor. Sung by The Dogmother. Released August 14, 2026 on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon, YouTube and everywhere music streams.
