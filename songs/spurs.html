The Story Behind This Song

He hung his spurs up years ago, after one too many rodeos. The dog rode shotgun through all of it — barroom dust, barbed-wire nights, the wild years.

Now it's a rocking chair, a Bible by the shotgun, grey in his muzzle and grey in the chin. Slower days, softer miles, and the kind of peace you earn.
