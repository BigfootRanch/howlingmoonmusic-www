The Story Behind This Song

There's a special kind of funny when somebody claims they're done with your life — but somehow keeps watching it. Wish You Were Me came from that energy: the ex who had what you have, destroyed it, moved on… and still can't stop scrolling to see how good he's doing without her.

This isn't heartbreak. It's a smirk. She can watch the love, the life, the music and every damn thing we built together from the front row. Hate me, study me, compare yourself to me — whatever gets you through the night. At some point jealousy starts looking a whole lot like admiration with an attitude.

Careful, girl. Keep watching this hard and people might think you wish you were me.
