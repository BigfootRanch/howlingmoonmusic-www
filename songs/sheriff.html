The Story Behind This Song

Sheriff came from being told, essentially, you don't get access anymore — by somebody who appointed himself gatekeeper and tried to back that control with authority and intimidation.

But there's one problem with trying to silence a songwriter.

You can control a phone. Block a page. Delete a message. Stand at a doorway. You cannot put handcuffs on a song.

And when the complaint became "her music keeps coming and coming and coming," he accidentally wrote the hook.

Damn right it keeps coming.

Every song is another flare over the wall. Another breadcrumb. Another way of saying: I'm still here.

So shine that fake Sheriff Spanky badge. Demand silence. Demand submission. Demand control.

The music keeps coming.
