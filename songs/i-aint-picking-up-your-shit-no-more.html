The Story Behind This Song

Is she talking to a man… or the dog?

Somebody’s been leaving messes, ignoring boundaries, making excuses and expecting her to clean it all up.

She’s had enough.

I Ain’t Picking Up Your Shit No More lives right on that dangerous little line where a bad relationship and bad house-training start sounding suspiciously alike.

Dropped it. Left it. Thought I’d handle it again?

Not this time.

And as for who she’s singing to?

That depends on which kind of shit you’ve been picking up.
