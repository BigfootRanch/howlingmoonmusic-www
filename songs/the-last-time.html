The Story Behind This Song

The cruel thing about "the last time" is that you usually don't know it's the last time while it's happening.

The last conversation. The last hug. The last ordinary moment before everything changes. If you had known, maybe you would have held on longer, listened harder, memorized every detail.

The Last Time lives inside that terrible hindsight. It's for anyone who keeps replaying a moment because they never got a proper goodbye — and would give almost anything to go back and understand that this ordinary little moment was about to become a memory.
