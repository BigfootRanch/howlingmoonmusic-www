The Story Behind This Song

Get Stoned With You sounds like a party song, but underneath it is really a long-marriage love song.

It's about making it through another week together — work boots by the door, payday already spoken for, bodies that don't bounce back quite like they used to, responsibilities that never seem to end — and finally getting to shut the whole damn world out for a while.

Because after you've raised babies, buried dreams, survived hard seasons, paid bills, laughed when there wasn't much else to laugh about, and somehow kept choosing each other, romance starts looking a little different.

Sometimes the perfect date is nowhere fancy.

It's just you, the person who made it through the mess beside you, nowhere you have to be tomorrow morning, and permission to finally let go.

The world can wait. Tonight, I just want to get stoned with you.
