The Story Behind This Song

Birthdays are supposed to be happy. Estrangement can turn them into mile markers.

Happy Birthday is about loving somebody on a day when you may not get to call them, hug them, hear their voice or know whether your message even reaches them. The calendar remembers even when relationships go silent.

So you celebrate them from wherever you are. You remember the birthdays that came before. You wonder what their life looks like now. And underneath everything is the same impossible sentence:

Happy birthday. I still love you. I wish I could tell you myself.
