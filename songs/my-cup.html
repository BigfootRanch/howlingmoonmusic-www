The Story Behind This Song

You can spend years pouring into everybody else and suddenly realize there's almost nothing left for you.

My Cup is about learning that replenishing yourself isn't selfish. Love, family, responsibility and survival can empty a person one drop at a time — especially when you become so accustomed to giving that you stop noticing how thirsty you are.

This song is a reminder to turn some of that care inward.

You cannot endlessly pour love from an empty cup.

Fill yours too.
