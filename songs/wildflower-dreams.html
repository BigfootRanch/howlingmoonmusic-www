The Story Behind This Song

Wildflower Dreams is about looking back at the person you were before life had a chance to rearrange all your plans.

Remember when you were young enough to believe you knew exactly where you were going?

There were dreams, big plans, impossible expectations and a whole wild world waiting outside the door.

Then life happened.

Some dreams came true. Some disappeared. Some changed shape so completely you barely recognize them anymore. And somehow you wake up years later in a life you never could have predicted — maybe sitting beside the sea, looking backward and wondering how in the hell you got here.

But Wildflower Dreams isn't a song about regret.

That's the point.

The road wandered. So did we.

We loved, lost, raised families, screwed things up, survived things we never expected and became people our younger selves couldn't possibly have imagined.

And after all of it?

I wouldn't change a thing.

Maybe wildflowers were never supposed to grow in straight lines.

Maybe neither were we.
