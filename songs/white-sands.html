The Story Behind This Song

WHITE SANDS uses the desert itself as a metaphor for love and time. The harder you close your hand around sand, the faster it slips away — but open your hand and some of it stays. Beneath the hypnotic “ticking” and “slipping” hook is a simple truth: love was never meant for a closing hand. Time keeps moving, so love while you can.
