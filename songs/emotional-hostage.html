The Story Behind This Song

A relationship doesn't have to be physically present to keep you emotionally trapped.

Emotional Hostage is about living on breadcrumbs — waiting for the call, the answer, the acknowledgment, the tiny sign that maybe today the door will open again. Hope can become its own prison when someone else controls when you receive it.

Eventually you realize you've handed another person the keys to your emotional life.

This song is about taking them back.

You can love somebody deeply and still refuse to spend the rest of your life standing outside a locked door.

Love shouldn't require captivity.
