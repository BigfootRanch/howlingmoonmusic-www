The Songs

Some people vacation at the beach. We live there in our heads. This is the music for sand between your toes, salt in your hair, and a sunset that makes you forget you have a real life to get back to. From the Texas coast to tropical fantasy, these songs are your one-way ticket to somewhere the phone doesn't ring.

01
WOKE UP LAUGHING

Some mornings you wake up and realize the nightmare is over. The person who hurt you is gone. The weight is lifted. And you just... laugh. This song is that first morning of freedom.

02
MANGROVES & MOONLIGHT

Southern heat. Salt water. No rules. The kind of night where the mangroves whisper and the moonlight tells secrets. Tropical soul music.

03
ENDLESS SUMMER HEAT
04
DOWN UNDER

This song isn't about Australia and it sure as hell isn't about surfing. Down under the covers. That's where this lives. Peaches, body heat, lights low, world gone. I wrote it on a night when the only place I wanted to be was tangled up with someone who made me forget my own name.

05
COCONUT KISS

Tropical, sweet, and gone too fast. Like a kiss that tastes like coconut and sunscreen on a beach that doesn't have a name. Summer fling in a song.

06

09
WORLD MELTS AWAY

We were outlaws from the start — them talking in corners, us playing cool till we couldn't anymore. Then you took my hand one sunset and we never looked back. I wrote this about the love that makes the whole world dissolve — road trips from the Smokies to the Gulf, barefoot dancing in moonlight, coconut air and salt on the breeze.

10
BOOTY BOOM BOOM

I wrote this because my dogs literally do a full-body wiggle dance every time I walk through the door. Tail going, butt going, whole body vibrating like they're gonna explode from happiness. I watched them one day and thought — that's a song. That's the purest party on earth.

11
COTTON CANDY

Soft. Sweet. Gone before you know it. This song is about the kind of love that dissolves — beautiful while it lasted, impossible to hold onto. From the county fair to the beach to silver in our hair, it traces a whole love story through the taste of something that melts the second it touches your tongue.

12

13
BAREFOOT BEACH BEAUTY

JT wrote this one — about the woman who doesn't need heels, makeup, or anyone's permission to be magnetic. Barefoot on the sand, salt in her hair, completely untouchable. Beach beauty isn't a look. It's an energy.

14
SAND IN TOES

The best days leave sand in your toes. No shoes, no plans, no worries — just the beach and whatever comes next. I wrote this on a day when the only thing on my to-do list was nothing, and it turned out to be the most productive day of my life.
