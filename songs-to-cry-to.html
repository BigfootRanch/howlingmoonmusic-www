The Songs

This is the playlist you play when you can't stop crying and don't want to. Every song here was written from the floor — literally or emotionally. Coffee that tastes like tears, phone calls you almost made, and the kind of shame that only heartbreak teaches you.

01
TEARDROPS IN MY COFFEE
02
HAZY BUBBLE

That floating feeling when reality softens at the edges and nothing hurts anymore. A hazy bubble of peace in the middle of the chaos. I wrote this during a time when I needed to disappear for a minute — not from life, but from the sharpness of it. Sometimes survival means letting the world go blurry.

03
COFFEE CONSTELLATION

Written at 4 AM staring at the coffee swirling in the cup and seeing the universe in it. When you can't sleep and the night feels infinite, even a cup of coffee becomes a constellation. I think the best songs come from insomnia — when the world goes quiet and the only thing louder than your thoughts is the drip of the coffee maker.

04
ROCK A BYE BABY

A raw, unflinching lullaby that cuts deep. This isn't the nursery rhyme you remember — it's the one you sing when the cradle has already fallen and you're picking up the pieces in the dark. For every mother rocking herself to sleep because her arms are empty.

05
ALMOST CALLED

2 AM. Phone in hand. Thumb hovering over the name you swore you'd delete. You almost called — and that almost is the whole story. This song is for everyone who's been one tap away from undoing all the healing they fought for.

06
THE LAST TIME
07
HAPPY BIRTHDAY
08
DROWNING IN MY TEARS

I wrote this on a night I couldn't stop crying. Not the pretty kind — the kind where your whole body shakes and you can't breathe and you're screaming at someone who isn't even listening.

09
NOT ENOUGH BOTTLES

When the pain is bigger than the liquor store. There aren't enough bottles in the world for some heartbreaks. So you stop drinking and start writing songs instead.

10
BROKEN HEART AND BUSTED DREAMS
11
IF TEARS COULD CALL HEAVEN

For everyone who's lost someone and wished they could just make one more call. If tears had a phone number, heaven's line would always be busy. This one's for my people on the other side.
