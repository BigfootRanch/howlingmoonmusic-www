The Story

Healing isn’t always a breakthrough.

Sometimes it is one unclenched jaw. One open hand. One night when your mind finally stops fighting long enough to rest. Sometimes it is admitting you cannot change what happened—but you can decide what continues living inside you.

These eight meditative songs move through breath, water, prayer, scripture, wooden flute, rain, ocean, drum and repeated mantras. They were created as a place to pause: to release the battle, make room for love, remember what sustains you and practice putting down what you were never meant to carry forever.

Nothing to prove. Nothing to solve right now.

Breathe. Receive. Release. Rest.

The Frequencies

01
BREATHE
“You Don’t Have to Fix Your Whole Life in This Breath.”

When everything becomes too loud—the grief, the anger, the thoughts colliding inside your own head—come back to the one thing still happening without your permission.

Breathe in. Breathe out.

There is nothing to fix in this moment. Nothing to know. Nothing to carry. Nowhere else you need to go.

This song is the landing place I needed when I could no longer hold myself in the air.

02
FLOATING
“Fear Was Passing Through. It Was Never You.”

Fear makes everything feel permanent: the weight, the pursuit, the certainty that something is about to fall apart.

This song loosens that grip.

There is nothing chasing you here. No ground you have to reach. No destination demanding your arrival. Only sky, open water and enough space to remember that fear is something you experience—not who you are.

Let it thin. Let it clear. Let yourself float.

03
HEALING WATERS
“I Stopped Asking, ‘How Could They?’ and Asked, ‘What Do I Need to Heal?’”

After enough anger and grief, you eventually become tired of carrying the poison.

This isn’t forgiveness that pretends nothing happened. It is a head-to-toe prayer for release—quieting the mind, unclenching the jaw, softening the heart and letting the weight move out of every place the body learned to hold it.

Water moves and changes shape without losing what it is.

Wash over me. Restore my soul. Carry away what I no longer need.

04
MY CUP
“The Prayer Wasn’t for More. It Was for Enough—and the Wisdom to Carry It.”

This is not simply a reminder to practice self-care. It is a prayer about provision without panic.

Open my hands. Restore my mind. Give me wisdom for every decision, discernment for every door and peace during every season of waiting. Establish the work of my hands and let what I build bear good fruit.

Not only money, but purpose. Not only opportunity, but wisdom. Enough for me. Enough to share. Enough to bless.

My cup runs over—and what comes to me can flow through me.

05
FILL ME WITH LOVE
“I Wanted Love to Become Louder Inside Me Than What Hurt Me.”

After enough pain, bitterness can begin occupying every room inside you.

This prayer asks for something different. Not revenge. Not forgetting. Not pretending the wounds never happened. It asks God to fill every place fear once lived with love, light, wisdom and peace.

Fill my mind until it knows peace. Fill my heart until fear has nowhere left to hide. Let what heals me become kindness for somebody else.

I will not let what hurt me decide what lives inside me.

06
NO FEAR
“I Don’t Need Revenge. I Need Refuge.”

Fear loves to disguise itself as protection. It tells you to watch every enemy, expose every lie and prepare for the next attack.

This song releases the battle.

I don’t have to chase it. I don’t have to repay it. I don’t have to leave the table God prepared for me just to fight with everyone who wanted me gone. Let every scheme collapse beneath the weight of truth without taking my peace with it.

I forgive—but I do not fear. The battle is not mine.

07
PEACE
“I Can Stop Carrying It Even If They Never Pick It Up.”

Peace is different when you had to fight your way back to it.

It doesn’t require the apology you deserved, the explanation you never received or one final argument rehearsed perfectly enough to make somebody understand. Peace begins when their behavior no longer controls the weather inside you.

What has passed, let it pass. What may come, let it come.

Nothing to prove. Nothing to solve. No fear. No hurry. No noise.

Peace, return to me.

08
SLEEP
“My Body Was Exhausted. My Mind Wouldn’t Let Me Rest.”

After the court battles, gaslighting and nights spent replaying every fight, sleep stopped coming.

My body was begging for rest while my mind kept standing guard—staring at the ceiling, reliving the worst moments and trying to solve things that could not be solved at three in the morning.

So I made the song I needed to hear.

No more thinking now. You don’t have to hold it all tonight. Let the thoughts pass without following them. Let the day release you.

Heavy eyes. Soft and slow.

Time to sleep.
